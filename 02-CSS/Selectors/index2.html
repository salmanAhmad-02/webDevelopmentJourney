Hyderabadi Chicken Dum Biryani - Authentic Step-by-Step Recipe

Hyderabadi dum biryani is loved for its rich taste and strong aroma. Being amongst the most searched recipes in India, it is also one of the most attempted and failed ones at home. Many of us try to make it at home, but the results do not always turn out as expected.

The rice may become soft, or the chicken may lack flavour. In most cases, the problem comes down to two things. The chicken is not prepared well, or the rice is not suitable for dum cooking. Both steps need care and the right approach.

This guide explains the full Hyderabadi chicken dum biryani recipe in a simple way. It covers how to choose the right rice, prepare the chicken, parboil the grains, layer properly, and cook on dum. Follow each step, and you can make an authentic Hyderabadi biryani at home with better results.

Ingredients You Need

To get the right taste, you need good-quality ingredients. Each one plays an important role in flavour and texture

  • 2 cups extra long grain aged basmati rice (Kohinoor Classic Gold works well)
  • 500 g chicken (bone-in pieces)
  • 1 cup thick yoghurt
  • 2 large onions (thinly sliced and fried)
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 3-4 green chillies
  • Whole spices (bay leaf, cloves, cardamom, cinnamon)
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves
  • ½ cup milk with saffron strands
  • 3 tbsp ghee
  • Salt as required
